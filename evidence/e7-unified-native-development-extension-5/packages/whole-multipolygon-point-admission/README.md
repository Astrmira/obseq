# Whole planar MultiPolygon point predicates: finite-contact admission and a numerical stress

The held-out GeoPackage component tests did not verify whole geometry. This project tests an explicit admissible subset of stored 2D MultiPolygons on an installed pinned QGIS map. It separates mathematical composition, external coverage, authored contact controls and native floating-point conformance. It is not a full validity checker for every permitted polygon representation.

## Exact composition and how to verify its premise

For a point, Intersects with a literal finite union is always OR of component Intersects. For strict Contains, assume every component is a regular closed planar polygon made of a simple shell and individually simple disjoint, non-nested holes; distinct interiors are disjoint, and pairwise boundary intersection is finite. These are compatible with the multipart rules in the [OGC Simple Features common architecture](https://docs.ogc.org/is/06-103r3/06-103r3.pdf); our inner ring checks deliberately admit a stricter subset.

Any component interior lies in union interior. Conversely, a union point not in a component interior lies on at least one component boundary. In every neighborhood of such a point choose a point q on that component's boundary avoiding its finitely many contacts with other boundaries. It cannot lie in another component's interior: any neighborhood of q contains interior points of its own regular component, contradicting disjoint interiors. Thus q is outside all other components. Since finitely many other components are closed, some neighborhood of q misses all of them. There are exterior points of the chosen component arbitrarily near q, hence exterior points of the whole union arbitrarily near the original point. The original point is not in union interior. In this exact planar scope, therefore, strict point Contains is OR of strict component Contains, including isolated vertex and vertex-edge contacts.

Admission parses original raw WKB and treats stored binary64 coordinates as exact rationals. Verify rings independently; reject zero-length/collinear adjacent edges, ring self-intersection, hole nesting and any within-component ring contact, and bound work by 420 segments and 12 parts. For distinct components reject proper transverse crossings and positive-length shared edges. Split every boundary edge at exact contact points and verify the midpoint of every remaining open piece is outside the other component. This last test also handles containment/tangency with overlapping interiors, which edge crossing alone would miss. A boundary piece cannot switch classification without another intersection. If interiors still overlapped, connectedness of one interior plus absence of the other's boundary inside it would force that entire interior to lie in the other; some non-contact boundary point would then lie inside the other, contradicting the piece check. Native IsValid is withheld until after admission and is not oracle input.

## External and authored outcomes kept separate

The fixed held-out cohort is the next 92 non-null country rows with FID >118 of installed QGIS world_map.gpkg SHA-256 386bc75f4ac46c329cdc44caff01e2e27385d3d89ca3ded26495090def11f669. Scan all in binary FID order; admit a whole MultiPolygon or nothing. Probe every admitted contact-bearing geometry and the first four disjoint admitted geometries. Exact gate admits 23 whole features, **none with an inter-component contact**. Exclusions: 65 scope limits, 3 unqualified rings, and 1 unsupported wrapper. Disjoint whole features FID 146/147/152/157 yield 1,479 exact stored-coordinate probes; native whole and OR-of-native-components both agree with independent exact truth. Both input and layer are read-only, and coordinates of every reopened native ring are matched to raw source. This is external whole-geometry evidence for disjoint components only. No real contact probe was identified under this frozen gate.

Authored valid controls separately exercise corner touch, vertex-to-edge T touch, and an island touching the hole boundary of another component. At all three isolated contacts the exact and native outcomes are (Contains false, Intersects true). Also reject a shared edge, proper crossing, nesting, and tangent nesting. The shared-edge example is **not** admitted as a MultiPolygon; its separately authored valid dissolved rectangle contains the edge seam where neither undissolved component does. That contrast explains the required precondition, not a native compatibility result on invalid input.

On the valid T-contact fixture, the smallest positive binary64 subnormal in the y direction from (1,0) gives an exact interior point. The OGR point round-trips the exact input bit patterns (float.hex). On GDAL 3.10.3 / GEOS 3.13.1 both native whole and native component OR instead return (false,true). They agree with each other while both disagreeing with exact truth. The exact contact, smallest-normal interior displacement, and regular exterior probes agree with their exact outcomes; power-of-two scaling of the exact failing geometry/point also passed the native interior check in this control. The cause was not instrumented, scaling is not a general remedy, and this authored subnormal is not reported as source prevalence or as a composition-specific error.
